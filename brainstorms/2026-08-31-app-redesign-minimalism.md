# App Complete Redesign: Brainstorm & Discovery Notes
Date: 2026-08-31 · Goal: Transform the couple finance tracker from a cluttered/childish UI into a razor-sharp, clever, minimalist financial instrument inspired by high-end design benchmarks.

## Summary / key decisions (Final Reconciled)

- **Core Product Identity**: 
  - **Single Purpose**: **Couple Total Available Cash App** — tracking real-time household spendable cash, daily burn rate runway, Force Gym business inflows, upcoming fixed deadlines, credit card batch statement parsing, and category distribution.
  - **Scope Cut**: Completely removed savings goals / dream travel jars.
- **Visual & Aesthetic Identity**:
  - **Foundation**: Luxury Dark Obsidian-Forest Slate (#080C0A / #0F1512 / #131B17), Swiss/Industrial typographic precision from Images 4 & 5.
  - **Malu Pastel Suite**:
    - *Pastel Mint/Sage*: #C4E8D1 (Inflows / Cash positive)
    - *Pastel Signal Blue*: #C7DBF8 (Galicia / Corporate / Base salary)
    - *Pastel Gold/Champagne*: #FBE4A0 (Force Gym dues / Member check-ins)
    - *Pastel Lilac/Lavender*: #DED2F9 (Fixed commitments / Subscriptions)
    - *Pastel Peach/Coral*: #FBC3B8 (Discretionary outflows / Warnings)
- **Typography & Telemetry Styling**:
  - High-impact tabular numbers with decimal formatting ($1,894.0 with .0 in muted tone).
  - Tonal solid rectangular bento blocks, micro-receipt gridlines, and receipt styling.
  - Avant-Garde Visualizations: Radial Frequency Sunburst / Rose Petal Category Breakdown and clean daily safe-burn runway.
- **Couple Cashflow & Attribution**:
  - Unified Household Pool: Master hero is the single combined available cash balance ($X,XXX.0 AVAILABLE).
  - Subtle payer tags ([ Mati ] / [ Belu ] / [ Joint ]).
  - Top filter pill switcher ([ ALL ] / [ MATI ] / [ BELU ]).
- **Navigation (3 Razor-Sharp Tabs)**:
  1. **Cockpit (Home)**: Statement Hero ($X,XXX.0 AVAILABLE) + Daily Safe Burn Runway + Bento Inflows/Outflows + Fixed Deadlines Radar + Recent Activity ledger.
  2. **Force Gym**: 1-Tap regular member dues check-in dial (Lucas, Camila, Gonzalo...) + Monthly revenue milestone + Supplement sales.
  3. **Analytics & Tools**: Category Sunburst / Petal breakdown + Credit Card smart batch parser + Dólar Blue live converter.
- **Fast-Capture Power Hub (+)**:
  - Tapping central + opens a 3-tile bento action sheet:
    1. Log Expense / Manual Inflow
    2. 1-Tap Gym Member Dues Check-in
    3. Paste Credit Card Statement (auto-split batch parser)
- **Pruning & Cleanup**:
  - ❌ Completely pruned: Miss Minutes cartoon mascot, Loki TVA references, 3D perspective tilt cards, cartoon avatar graphics, weather widget, photo background slideshow, and savings goals.

## Q&A log

### Q1 — Visual Direction & Palette Identity
- Asked: Aesthetic choice between OLED Stealth, Swiss Sage Industrial, or Luxury Dark Forest.
- Captured: Selected **Option C (Luxury Dark Forest & Tactile Cards)**, customized with **Malu brand pastel colors** for all accents/charts.

### Q2 — Home Screen Hero & Information Architecture
- Asked: Hero hierarchy between Stacked Cards + Wave vs Radial Arc Cockpit vs Modular Bento Command Center.
- Captured: Selected **Option 3 (Modular Bento Command Center)**, emphasizing that **Images 4 & 5 are the user's favorite aesthetics** (industrial typography, decimal styling, radial frequency sunburst, tonal rectangular blocks, receipt micro-lines).

### Q3 — Feature Pruning & Navigation Structure
- Asked: Pruning list (Miss Minutes, 3D tilt, avatars, weather) and 3-tab vs 4-tab structure.
- Captured: Selected **Option A (3 Tabs)**, approved full pruning of childish elements, and explicitly decided to **skip savings goals completely** as this app is specifically a **Couple Total Available Cash App**.

### Q4 — Couple Cashflow Model & Attribution
- Asked: Individual vs shared spending handling.
- Captured: Selected **Option 1 (Unified Household Pool + Subtle Payer Attribution)**. Single joint available cash metric with subtle [ Mati ] / [ Belu ] / [ Joint ] stamps and an ALL / MATI / BELU filter.

### Q5 — Fast Capture UX & Quick-Action Hub (+)
- Asked: Keypad sheet vs Multi-Action Power Hub.
- Captured: Selected **Option B (Multi-Action Power Hub)** with 3 direct bento tiles: Quick Expense/Income, 1-Tap Gym Dues, and Paste Credit Card Statement.
